Role Permissions Overview

👑 Super Admin

Full unrestricted platform control. Can manage all users, assign any role, reset credentials, access and edit any company game, and reassign ownership.

Admin

Standard company owner. Can create and manage their own companies and games. Cannot view or edit other users' companies or access super admin tools.

Staff

Operational member. Can view assigned company dashboard and claim codes, and mark gifts as handed out. Cannot alter game wheel settings or delete companies.

User

Standard account. Can log in and view games.
